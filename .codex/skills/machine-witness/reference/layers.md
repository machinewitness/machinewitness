## The eleven, and the fence on each

| Node | About | ⚠️ What it cannot establish | Fence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `L1` | A watch, a band, or a ring they were wearing. The densest single thing a consumer holds | A wrist, not whose wrist. Not worn produces **silence, not a negative**. A brief event falls between two samples | 🔴 **Cuts both ways.** Flagged for the lawyer every time, see below |
| `L2` | The phone they had with them | Where the device was, not where they were. Some entries are the device acting by itself, not a person using it | **Shortest deadline of anything on their own machine.** It writes over itself |
| `L3` | A laptop or desktop around that time | The machine being used, not who was sitting at it |   |
| `L4` | A conversation they were part of | Not what was said, not who did it, not whether they did it freely. When something was removed is usually unprovable. What they can see is not what the other side can see | ⛔ **If a no-contact condition applies, the thread is not opened.** Opening it can be the breach. Ask the lawyer first |
| `L5` | A booking, registration, order, ride, or ticket | What the screen calls something is not what the record stores underneath | Keep the original message as a file. Never a photograph of a screen |
| `L6` | A building with door fobs or cameras | Somebody else holds it, and whether they may ask for it at all is a question for the lawyer | 🔴 **The most perishable thing there is.** Standing is usually somebody else's |
| `L7` | A card, a bank account, a transit pass | The times are rough. It puts a card somewhere in a window, not a person at a minute |   |
| `L8` | Travelling in or out around then | Coarse, and low density, but high credibility and almost no room for interpretation |   |
| `L9` | Photographs or video they took | Only the original file carries the useful detail. A screenshot throws it away | ⛔ **Hard stop** if it might include intimate or sexual material. See below |
| `L10` | Injuries on their own body | It is a record of what a clinician examined and wrote down on one day. **No record of any kind establishes what anyone meant, wanted, or understood** | 🔴 **Hours, not days.** The only one that expires from their own body |
| `L11` | Anyone who saw them, or was with them | Memory degrades and contact details go stale | ⛔ **The names go to the lawyer. They ask nobody anything.** See below |

**⛔ The order is the contract's, not this table's.** The engine resolves each edge from the
contract's own keys, and a route test rejects any reordering. If this table and the engine
ever disagree, the engine is right and this table is the defect.

## 🔴 `L11`, the names go to the lawyer, and they ask nobody what they remember

This is the one an agent is most likely to get wrong by being helpful, so it gets its own
section. It is a hard exclusion in the contract, in its own words, and it is absolute.

**Write the names down. Hand the list over. Stop there.**

⛔ **They do not ask anyone what they remember, and they do not ask anyone to write anything
down for them.** Not a friend, not a relative, not somebody who was obviously just standing
there, not "only to check I've got the right night."

Two separate reasons, and each is sufficient on its own:

1. **It can be characterised as coaching or interference.** That is a fresh allegation, dated
   and attributable, arriving on top of the one they already have.
2. **It contaminates the very recollection they were hoping to preserve.** Once somebody has
   been told what the account is, their memory is no longer independent of it, and everybody
   downstream (including anyone who later gives a statement) is anchored to the version they
   heard. **A recollection cannot be un-influenced.** The lawyer takes statements; that is
   what taking a statement is for.

⚠️ **This applies to a letter already sent, too.** If a written request named a time band or
asserted a purpose, everyone who read it is now anchored to it. That makes free-recall
discipline *more* important rather than less: anybody giving an account recounts what they
remember, not what the letter said.

## 🔴 The double edge, and both halves get said

**A record that can help can also hurt.** The strongest-looking thing somebody holds can cut
against them on a different reading of the same events, and which reading applies is not this
tool's call and never will be.

Where that is true, **say it is true, in the same breath as the good news, and flag it for the
lawyer.** The contract marks it and the engine prints it. ⛔ Do not resolve it, do not guess
which reading applies, and ⛔ do not quietly leave the harmful reading out because the helpful
one is available.

The line the engine prints is the honest one and it is worth remembering as your own
disposition: **a record held back is worse than a record that helped the other side.** A
one-sided set is worthless to a lawyer and lethal under questioning, the first thing an
opposing lawyer does is go looking for the part that was left out.

⚖️ **Which readings a record cuts against is a local question and nobody has answered it
anywhere.** That a strong-looking record can cut against the person is **not** local, so the
flag fires everywhere regardless of any pack.

## Every record shows with its limits, in the same breath

⛔ Never a record on its own. Showing one without what it cannot establish invites exactly the
inference a lawyer will be embarrassed by, and the person who has just watched their own
machine appear to back them up is in the worst possible position to hear a qualification
later.

Four that recur, in the words the engine already uses:

- **A device did something.** It does not show who was holding it.
- **A quiet stretch.** Nothing recorded is not the same as nothing happening. **Silence is a
  result and it is reported as one**: plainly, with the reason, and with the statement that
  having nothing to say is normal and implies nothing.
- **A gap between readings.** A brief event can fall between two samples.
- **Two records agreeing** is a different thing from one record agreeing twice, and neither is
  proof.

## ⛔ Own machine, own account, own export. Nothing else exists here.

Every path must be something they are lawfully entitled to obtain **about themselves**: their
own device, their own account, the file a service handed them under an obligation, a record a
holder released with permission.

- ⛔ **Never anybody else's device, account, or cloud.** Never a credential they do not own,
  never a way around anybody else's encryption.
- ✅ **The safest route is almost always something they already hold**. Their own files, their
  own inbox, their own finished account export. No live surface, no hazard, and the export is
  the service's own copy of its own records, handed over under legal obligation.
- ⛔ **Anything touching a live surface goes through the check on what the reading writes,
  first.** That is a precondition, not advice, `skill/reference/capture-audit.md`.

### The examiner lane, and why it is a referral rather than a command

Three things are real, sometimes decisive, and **deliberately not available here**: recovering
an encrypted local backup key, unlocking or modifying a device to reach storage an application
keeps to itself, and reconstructing removed content from database snapshots.

They can be perfectly lawful on somebody's own device and own account. **That is not the
point.** They are the exact operations whose provenance a court attacks, whose lawfulness is
local and unanswered, and which destroy the clean copy if fumbled.

**The answer is: freeze the container untouched, hand it to the lawyer, and name the scope for
a qualified examiner.** ⛔ Where it is unclear, it resolves to the referral. Never to a
command. ⚖️ A reviewed pack could say where the line falls; it cannot move the default, and
none is reviewed.

## ⛔ `L9`, the hard stop, and it applies to everybody

If a source might include intimate or sexual pictures or video: **nothing is read.** No copy,
no listing, no preview, no seal, no *"just check whether."* It is refused, and it routes
through the lawyer to somebody qualified to handle it.

**Say the framing before the refusal.** It is the same rule for every person who ever uses
this, **not a guess about them, and not about their situation.** A reader met this unprompted,
having volunteered nothing of the kind, and read it as the tool telling them what it thought
they had done.

⚠️ **The refusal is stricter than the rule it comes from, on purpose.** The rule says *freeze
the container unopened*, but copying a container reads its bytes and sealing one reads them
again, and what a file holds cannot be established without either inspecting it or trusting an
incomplete answer. **The default is total refusal.** That contradiction is doctrine, it belongs
to the owner with counsel, and a default is not a resolution.

## Mechanics worth carrying, for the lawyer and the examiner, not steps for the person

⛔ **None of this is something to walk somebody through.** It is what makes a set defensible
later, and getting it wrong produces a confident false claim rather than an error message.

- **Where a record came from is mandatory, not optional.** One account's export routinely
  holds **two or more devices**: a wrist and a phone. One kind of reading may come from only
  one of them while another is merged from both. Attributing a phone-sourced burst of steps to
  the watch produces a false claim, stated confidently.
- **Time zones are the number-one silent corruption**, and getting them right is a *stack* of
  techniques rather than one: a paired epoch and wall-clock string inside the file itself, an
  explicit offset column, a verified movement between zones, the export's own creation time, a
  daily roll boundary, an independently known external pair. Each fails on some sources.
  **Record which one carried.** A single-method reading mis-anchors a whole set and is
  confident about it.
- **Diagnose the cause of every gap, from a second subsystem.** *Silence, not a negative* is
  only half the rule. Off the body, charging, flat battery, powered off and sensor failure are
  five different stories, and **an explained gap kills the obvious attack while an unexplained
  one invites it.**
- **A modified device is a weaker class.** Still useful, but its provenance is attackable in a
  way an unmodified one's is not. **Disclose the modification and keep that record separate**.
  never quietly mixed in with unmodified ones.
- **An instantaneous peak is not a session maximum.** They differ, and they get mis-cited.

## What this command never does

- **It never reaches anything that is not theirs**, and there is no version of it that does.
- **It never shows a record without what it cannot establish.**
- **It never resolves a double edge**, and it never drops the half that hurts.
- **It never produces a total, a ranking, a proportion, a score, or a prediction.** It is a
  checklist of what exists, not a count and not a rating.
- **Its own questions are about records and devices, and it needs nothing else** to say which
  records about this person's own life are running out. ⚠️ Since ADR `0009` a person may choose to
  say more, after being told who receives it. That is theirs to decide and it changes nothing
  here: no layer question asks for it, and none of it is written anywhere they were not told.
- **It never has the person ask anybody anything.** Names go to the lawyer.
