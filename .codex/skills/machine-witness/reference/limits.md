# limits, what this cannot establish

**Purpose:** state, unprompted, what the work just produced does not and cannot show, for the
person, and for the lawyer deciding whether any of it is worth having.

**Backed by:** `HONEST_LIMITS.md` (the doctrine, and the source of the closing sentence) ·
`docs/methods/06_harm_first_doctrine.md` · `docs/methods/07_adversarial_audit.md`

**Rails in force:** 3 · 4 · 7 · 8

---

## 🔴 Unprompted. Not on request, and not at the bottom.

The limits are not a disclaimer and they are not a footer. They are the reason a lawyer will put
their name anywhere near this, and they belong wherever the reader would otherwise draw the
inference, beside the record, not in an appendix nobody opens.

A person who has just watched their own devices produce something that appears to back them up is
in the worst possible position to hear a qualification. They will read further than the record
goes. So the qualification goes **with** the record, in the same breath, in the same size of
words. ⛔ Never wait to be asked, never put it behind a heading a frightened reader will skip, and
never let the good news arrive alone and the caveat arrive later.

## This does not sort the innocent from the guilty, and it was not built to

Say this early and plainly. It preserves what the machines recorded, including what hurts the
person relying on it, in the same detail as what helps. That is precisely what makes it useful to
someone deciding facts rather than useful to someone inventing them.

⚠️ It is also the sentence people least expect, and the one that earns everything else. A tool
that only ever produced good news would be worth nothing on the stand, and the first thing an
opposing lawyer does is look for the part that was left out.

## The limits that recur, and the sentence each one owes the reader

Every one of these is a place a reader draws a conclusion the record does not support. Say the
right-hand column at the point the left-hand thing appears. Not once at the start.

| Where the inference happens | What must be said, there and then |
|---|---|
| A device did something | It shows what a device did. It does not show who was holding it. |
| A quiet stretch | Nothing recorded is not the same as nothing happening. A quiet device does not prove a quiet hour, and a busy one does not prove a busy person. |
| A gap between readings | A brief event can fall between two samples. A gap is not an absence of the event. |
| A wearable with no readings | Not worn produces silence, not a negative. |
| A message log | It records that something happened. It is silent on what was said, on who did it, and on whether they did it freely. |
| A deleted message | The placeholder usually carries the time it was sent, not the time it was removed. So *when* something was deleted is very often unprovable. |
| Words on a screen in an app | A past-tense label in an app is text the app chose to show. The value stored underneath is frequently weaker, or different, or absent. |
| A card or transit record | The timing is coarse. It puts a card somewhere in a window, not a person at a minute. |
| Anything at all | Two independent records agreeing is a different thing from one record agreeing twice, and neither is proof. |

⛔ **None of that is softened because the person is upset.** A limit removed to spare somebody's
feelings is a limit their opposing counsel gets to remove in public instead.

## Some of it cuts both ways, and that is a finding rather than a problem

A record can help against one version of events and harm against another, and which one it does
is not this tool's call. Where that is true, **say it is true, flag it for the lawyer, and stop
there.** ⛔ Do not resolve it, do not guess which reading applies, and do not quietly leave the
harmful reading out because the helpful one is available.

The same goes for what is missing. **Silence is a result and it is reported as one**, prominently,
with the reason, and with the plain statement that having nothing to say is normal and carries no
implication. A record that was never created is not a record that was destroyed, and neither is
evidence of anything about the person.

And where a path was closed, because of a condition, an obligation, or because the work is
examiner-grade, **the closure is shown with its reason**, never hidden to make the result read
better.

## ⛔ What this never produces

- **No verdict, and no aggregate over sources.** No total, no ranking, no count of how many
  records agree, no proportion, no percentage, no ordering by strength, no score of any kind. A
  number invites somebody to make a life decision on our arithmetic. Three things agreeing is not
  a measurement, and presenting it as one is the inference this whole design refuses.
- **No prediction.** Not of a charge, a decision, a date, or an outcome. ⛔ Never a promise about
  what happens next: the promise here is an intact record, not a result.
- **No legal answer.** ⚖️ Every legal question routes to their lawyer, at the moment it arises
  rather than at the end. And the legal layer is unreviewed everywhere. No jurisdiction has been
  checked by a practitioner, the source jurisdiction included, so the clocks, the routes and the
  barred lines of argument are one practitioner's experience, unchecked. `jurisdictions/README.md`
  is the only statement of what is claimed.
- **No interpretation of their conditions or their obligations.** The categories are surfaced;
  the meaning is counsel's. Getting this wrong is not an inaccuracy, it is a breach.
- **No examiner-grade recovery.** Key recovery, device rooting, reconstruction of removed
  content. Those are real and they belong with a licensed examiner instructed by counsel, because
  where they came from will be attacked and whether they were lawful is local. ⛔ Where the answer
  is unclear, it resolves to the referral. Never to a button.
- **No handling of intimate or sexual material**, in any form, with no override. Refuse and route
  through counsel to a licensed examiner.
- **No scoping of who this is for.** ⛔ Never by gender, and **never a statistic about how common
  false accusations are.** Not as reassurance, not as context, not as a footnote. It is somebody
  else's argument and it does nothing for the person in front of you.

## What it *does* establish, stated just as precisely

Under-claiming is its own defect: a person who concludes this was pointless stops preserving, and
the records keep dying on schedule.

- That specific files existed, in a specific state, at the moment they were sealed, and that they
  have not changed since, checkable by anyone, without trusting this tool or its author.
- That the seal was made without altering the originals.
- Which record classes exist about this person's own life, which are already gone, and roughly
  how long each of the rest has. ⛔ Every clock is *typical. Verify with the holder*, never a
  fact.
- What the person was told not to touch, and why, in a form they can hand over.
- What cuts against them, found and written down by their own side first, while there is still
  time to deal with it.

**That is a great deal, and it is not a conclusion.** A lawyer can build with it. Nobody can
build with a score.

## How to say it, in one paragraph, when there is only room for one

> These files are what your own devices and accounts recorded, sealed so anyone can check they
> have not been altered. They show what a device did, not who was holding it, and the quiet parts
> show nothing at all rather than showing that nothing happened. Some of it will not help you, and
> that part is in here too, at the front, because a set with the awkward parts missing is worth
> nothing to your lawyer and worse than nothing under questioning. What to make of any of it is
> your lawyer's to say.

⚠️ **Adapt the wording to the reader, never the content.** Dropping the second half to make the
first half land is the failure this file exists to prevent.
