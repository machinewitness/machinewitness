# Code of conduct

## The standard

Review the work, not the person. Assume competence and good faith. Say what is wrong and why, plainly, without contempt.

## Specific to this project

**Nobody is asked to explain why they need this.** Contributors and users may be dealing with something they have no wish to discuss. Do not ask about anyone's circumstances, and do not speculate about them.

**Do not post real case material in an issue or pull request.** Not a name, a date, an address, a document, a screenshot, or an identifier, yours or anyone else's. A public issue tracker is a permanent, indexed, mirrored record, and something posted here cannot be recalled. Reproduce a bug with synthetic data; if you genuinely cannot, describe the shape of the problem and say so.

**Anonymity is respected.** Do not attempt to identify a contributor, and do not treat a pseudonym as a reason to discount the work.

**No gendered framing of who this is for.** It is for people facing an accusation. Framing it as being about men, or about any group, is inaccurate, excludes people who need it, and will be removed.

## Unacceptable

Harassment. Contempt for people asking basic questions. Attempts to identify a pseudonymous contributor. Posting real case material after being asked not to. Advocacy about the guilt or innocence of anyone, in any matter, anywhere in this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock content, and may block accounts. Real case material posted in the tracker is removed on sight without waiting for discussion, because the harm is immediate and grows with time.

Report by opening an issue, or by contacting a maintainer privately if the report should not be public.
