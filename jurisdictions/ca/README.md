> **Safe to rely on: No.** Read [`00_status.md`](00_status.md) before anything else in this directory.

This pack is a question list, not an answer sheet. It exists for two reasons: so a consumer asking *"may I use the Canadian pack?"* gets a definite **no** rather than finding no file and guessing, and so the first Canadian practitioner to look at this has somewhere specific to start.

## The uncomfortable part

The practitioner experience behind every document in [`../../docs/methods/`](../../docs/methods/) is Canadian, and almost none of it says so. Exactly one line in the entire method corpus names Canada. This pack is where those unmarked assumptions were supposed to be checked. **It has checked none of them.**

So being Canadian buys a reader nothing here. That the text was written from Canadian practice is not a review, and this pack is the difference. `00_status.md` § *Why an empty Canadian pack is the most misleading state in the project* says more.

## ⚠️ "National" is provisional and probably wrong for several files

Criminal procedure is federal. Release-condition practice, premises-record practice, privacy statutes and law-society advertising rules are provincial or territorial, and Quebec differs on more than language. Expect `ca/<province>/` sub-packs. **A reviewer's first job is to say which answers are good nationally and which are not**. That decides whether this stays one pack.

## If you can fill any of this

You need no permission, no account, and no identity, ADR [`0003`](../../docs/adr/0003-free-open-anonymous.md). Start with [`../_template/README.md`](../_template/README.md) for the rules, then work the ordered list in `00_status.md`.

Two that are hard and non-negotiable:

- **⛔ Do not guess, and do not let a model fill a cell.** `UNANSWERED` is a correct value. An invented answer is a defect, because someone will act on it.
- **⛔ Public authority only.** No matter you are working on, no client, no charge combination, no file number, no date window. ⚠️ A *set* of statute sections is a fingerprint even with no name attached.
