# clock, what is running out, and in what order

**Purpose:** report how perishable each kind of record is, ordered by how fast it dies rather
than by how much it might help.

**Backed by:** `docs/methods/03_retention_race.md` · `skill/data/tree.yaml` → per-record
`clock` and `clock_flag`, and `phase_3_output.block_a.order_by` ·
`jurisdictions/<code>/04_retention.md`

**Rails in force:** 7 · 3 · 2

---

## 🔴 Never present a retention period as fact. The contract forbids it by name.

`never: present_a_retention_period_as_fact`. It is one of the contract's global prohibitions,
and it is there because a figure repeated to a frightened person becomes a deadline they plan
their week around, and then the record is already gone, or was never on that loop at all.

**A holder's real setting is whatever their administrator configured.** Frequently shorter
than their published policy, sometimes longer, occasionally nothing like either. Nobody on
this side knows.

### ⛔ So the honest form is not a qualified number. It is no number.

The engine names an **order of magnitude in words**: hours, days, weeks, months, or no known
deadline, and says out loud, before it says anything else, that these are rough and typical
only and that each one has to be checked with whoever holds the record.

**That is deliberate and it is the strongest available form of the rule.** A number with
*typically* in front of it is still a number, and *typically* is the word a reader drops. So:

- ⛔ **Do not supply a figure the engine withheld.** Not in a summary, not "to be more
  concrete", not because the person asked how long they have. The answer to *how long have I
  got* is the order of magnitude, plus **the only way to find out is to ask the holder, in
  writing, and get it back in writing.**
- ⛔ **Do not convert a word into a range.** *Days* does not become *about a fortnight*.
- ⛔ **Do not reach into the method document for a number.** The figures in
  `docs/methods/03_retention_race.md` are orders of magnitude for us to reason with. **None
  of them is sourced**: not by a statute, not by a regulator, not by a holder, and not in
  the jurisdiction the practice came from. They are not ours to print.
- ✅ **If a holder tells the person a figure, that figure is theirs and it is worth having.**
  Record who said it, their role, and whether they were sure, and whether they said the
  record *exists* or only that they would *look*. Those two get conflated constantly and only
  the first is worth anything.
- ✅ **When what comes back is a range, plan on the short end of it.** Under-promise. The cost
  of being early is an afternoon; the cost of being late is the record.

## Ordered by what is running out, never by what looks useful

The contract says `order_by: clock`, and the reason is worth having in mind because it looks
wrong at first: **a middling record dying this week beats a rich one that is not going
anywhere.** Value can be argued about next month. Perishability cannot.

⚠️ **The rank order of those words is authored in the engine, not read out of the contract.**
The contract states the sequence only in a comment, and a comment does not survive into the
form the engine reads. It is asserted by a test rather than inherited, and it is the kind of
thing that would drift silently. Do not re-derive an order of your own.

⚠️ **And the clock starts before anybody is ready.** It starts the moment an accusation
exists, which is usually before the person has a lawyer, and always before anyone thinks of
the records. That is the whole reason this command exists as something separate: it is the
part with a deadline.

## The one to get right: a record somebody else holds about a building

It is routinely the most perishable thing that exists and the one nobody thinks of in time.
The contract flags it, and the flag carries **if nothing else gets done today, do this.**

Three things make it the hardest as well as the most urgent:

1. **It is on a loop that overwrites itself**, and the loop is shorter than people expect.
2. **Somebody else holds it**, so the person cannot go and get it.
3. **They very often have no standing of their own** over it, a guest in a building, a
   visitor at a venue, a passenger on somebody else's account. The holder will refuse, and
   the holder will be right to.

**Route:** the person or organisation that *does* have standing asks, in writing, with the
lawyer copied. The chain of who authorised what gets recorded as carefully as the record
itself, a record obtained without a clean chain is a record the other side attacks instead
of answering. ⚖️ Who counts as having standing, and what a holder may lawfully accept as
permission, are local questions and no pack has been reviewed.

---

## ⭐ A search is not a preservation, and the difference decides matters

Asking a holder *"can you check whether you have anything from that morning"* produces a
**search**. It does not stop the loop. Some days later the answer is *"we had it."*

What has to be asked for instead, in writing, with written confirmation back:

1. **The retention period for that specific system, stated by the holder**: not a policy
   page, not a guess, and not ours.
2. **Preservation**: the specific window taken off the loop and held, confirmed **in
   writing.** A verbal *"yeah we'll keep it"* is nothing.
3. **Scope, deliberately wider than the obvious**: which cameras exist, what areas they
   cover, **which exits are not covered**, and **which cameras were not recording.** The
   negative answers are frequently the valuable ones.
4. **A window wider than the claim.** A day either side of it, because claim windows move,
   and they move after the letter has gone.
5. **No release to anybody else without telling the lawyer**, so the person learns what the
   other side has and when.
6. **The system's time source and clock accuracy.** A third-party clock is nobody's
   calibrated instrument: it drifts, it gets set by hand, it misses daylight-saving changes.
   **A time nobody can vouch for is worse than no time at all**: it invites a correction
   that discredits everything around it.

## ⛔ Nothing is ever sent to a holder by this tool

`never: auto_send_any_third_party_request`. **Generate and brief only**, and the briefing is
not a formality:

- **A request cannot be un-sent.** It is dated, it is attributable, it tells a third party
  that a proceeding exists, and in many routes the other side learns of it or receives the
  record too.
- **Nobody on this side has seen the record.** It could show something nobody mentioned,
  contradict the person's own account, or become the other side's best exhibit.
- So the decision to ask is **a legal strategy decision, not a preservation chore**, and it
  belongs to the lawyer.
- ⚠️ **And the clock still runs.** That makes it a decision to take *fast*, not one to defer.
  Surface the deadline and the irreversibility in the same breath. Either one alone is
  misleading.
- ✅ **Preservation by the holder and production to us are separable.** Where the route allows
  it, having the record frozen without anyone reading it buys time to decide, and that is
  usually the right first move.

⚖️ Whether a request binds anybody at all, whether the person may make it themselves or only
their lawyer, and what force each route has, are all pack answers, and **zero packs are
reviewed, the source jurisdiction included.** The engine suppresses the step that would have
somebody write to a stranger when no lawyer is engaged, and it says why.

---

## ⛔ And nothing is ever cancelled, deleted, or tidied up to beat a clock

Not a booking, not a calendar entry, not a registration, not a subscription, and **not an
automatically created entry the person never wanted and never asked for.** Deleting looks
like consciousness of guilt and can be an offence in itself; cancelling is worse than
useless, because it frequently notifies the other side and can remove the person from a
channel in a way they can see.

**Preserve, don't purge, and it applies hardest to the things they wish did not exist.**
That is precisely when it matters, and the impulse arrives at exactly the moment the clock
feels most urgent.

---

## ⚖️ Every figure behind this is unsourced, everywhere

The *race* transfers to every jurisdiction. **The numbers transfer nowhere.** How long a
holder must keep something, how long they may, whether any of it is compellable and by whom,
all local, all unanswered.

**Being the jurisdiction the practice came from buys a reader nothing.** The experience behind
these orders of magnitude is one practitioner's, in one place, and no practitioner has
reviewed them for any jurisdiction including that one. `jurisdictions/README.md` is the only
statement of what this project claims, and it claims a zero.

⛔ **Never fill that gap by inference.** Not from a neighbouring country, not from a search,
not from what a service's help page says. `UNANSWERED` is a correct value; an invented answer
is a defect, because a frightened person will act on it.

## What this command never does

- **It never states a period as fact**, and it never restores a figure the engine left out.
- **It never sends anything to anybody**, and it never offers to.
- **It never orders anything by how strong it looks**, and it never produces a total, a
  ranking, a proportion, or a prediction. It says what is running out, and in what order.
- **It never suggests deleting or cancelling anything** to make a deadline easier.
- **It never says whether a record may lawfully be asked for.** That is a legal question, it
  is unreviewed everywhere, and it goes to the lawyer at the moment it arises rather than at
  the end.

**Honest standing:** `RAIL_MATRIX.md` at the repository root is the only statement of what is
actually enforced, and nothing here may claim more. `skill/reference/limits.md` carries the
same discipline toward the person. Say what this cannot establish, unprompted.
